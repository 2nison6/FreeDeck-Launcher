## Online services

FreeDeck contacts these two free services, with no account or API key, and sends nothing else to anyone:

| Service | Used for | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ipwho.is](https://ipwho.is) | Finding your approximate city from your IP address | Your public IP address (as with any web request) |
| [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) | Current weather | The approximate latitude and longitude of that city |

Open-Meteo's free service is for **non-commercial use** under its [terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) and
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) attribution (weather data by Open-Meteo.com). If you build a
commercial product from this code, get your own licence or switch to another weather source
(`app/src/main/java/com/nison/freedeck/data/weather/WeatherRepository.java`).

## Pictures generated by the project

The scene backgrounds (`scene_*.jpg`) and the dotted world map are produced by the scripts in `tools/`. The scenes are
drawn from shapes and gradients, and the map uses land outlines from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) (public domain). The app icon is the project's own.
